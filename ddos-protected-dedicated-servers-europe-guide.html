Understanding Volumetric & Application Layer DDoS Threats

Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks are increasing in scale and complexity. Modern cyber threats target network infrastructure via SYN floods, UDP amplification (Layer 3/4), and HTTP/HTTPS flood queries (Layer 7).

CloudHoste Anti-DDoS Mitigation Architecture

  • Always-On Automated Detection: Inspection engines continuously monitor incoming packet rates and packet signatures.
  • Sub-Second Traffic Scrubbing: Traffic is dynamically redirected to scrubbing centers in Frankfurt & Amsterdam within 3 seconds of an anomaly.
  • Zero Latency Overhead: Clean traffic is routed directly back over low-latency backbone links to your dedicated server or VPS.
