Data Sovereignty: Why EU Businesses are Leaving US Cloud Providers

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), European organizations handling customer metrics, financial records, or personal data must maintain strict control over data residency and third-party access. Recent legal rulings have established that relying on US-based cloud hyperscalers introduces risks under the US CLOUD Act.

Key Pillar Requirements for GDPR Compliant Hosting

  • 1. Physical EU Data Residency: Servers must be located inside European Union member states (e.g. Germany, Netherlands, Italy).
  • 2. ISO 27001 Security Standard: Data centers must hold active ISO 27001 certification with strict access control.
  • 3. No Extra-Territorial Data Access: Zero unauthorized data transfers to third countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA).
  • 4. Data Processing Agreements (DPA): Legally compliant DPA contracts provided for all hosting clients.
